# SciFi Helmet

Model by Michael Pavlovic; glTF conversion by Norbert Nopper (2017).
License: CC0 1.0 Universal.

Source: https://github.com/KhronosGroup/glTF-Sample-Assets/tree/main/Models/SciFiHelmet
License: https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/legalcode

The mesh is unmodified. Textures were resized to 2048px (base color and normal)
or 1024px (metallic/roughness and AO), converted to JPEG and referenced by the glTF.
The REC scene changes display scale, orientation and lighting. This is a fictional
prop used to illustrate exploration.
